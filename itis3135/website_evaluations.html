Website Evaluations

For this assignment I picked two websites, one I think is designed really well and one that is about as bad as it gets, and graded each of them on the four CRAP principles: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity.

Apple

  • Contrast: The product names are huge and bold, the tagline under them is smaller, and the availability line is smaller and gray, so I can tell right away what to read first. The "Learn more" button is solid blue and the second button is just an outline, which makes the main action obvious. The sections also switch between light gray and black backgrounds, so each product feels like its own thing.
  • Repetition: Every product block follows the same formula: name, short tagline, release date, two buttons, then a big product photo. They use the same font and the same blue the whole way down. After the first block I already knew how to read every other one, which is the point.
  • Alignment: Everything lines up on one center line. The headline, tagline, buttons, and photo all stack on the same axis, and the nav bar is spaced evenly across the top. Centering usually isn't great for reading, but it works here because none of the text is longer than a line or two.
  • Proximity: The tagline and buttons sit right under the product name, so it's clear they belong together, and there's a lot of empty space between products so they don't blend into each other. Even the footer is grouped into columns by topic (shopping, account, store, and so on) instead of one giant list of links.

Arngren

  • Contrast: Everything on this page is trying to be loud at the same time, so nothing actually stands out. There's red, blue, green, and black text, a lot of it bold, and prices are in red next to headlines that are also red. Some of the small captions are thin gray text on white, which is hard to read. My eyes didn't know where to go first.
  • Repetition: There's basically no pattern. Each product box has a different font size, color, and layout. Some captions are above the picture, some are below it, and some are squeezed off to the side. It feels like every item was added on a different day by a different person.
  • Alignment: There's no grid at all. Pictures of totally different sizes are jammed together, columns are uneven, and text runs right up against the edges of images. The links in the left sidebar are all different colors and sizes too, so even the menu doesn't line up.
  • Proximity: This is the worst part. Prices and captions are crammed so close to the items next to them that I honestly couldn't always tell which price went with which scooter. Electric bikes, ATVs, and drones are all mixed together with no space between categories. The site is in Norwegian and I don't speak it, but the layout would be confusing in any language.
