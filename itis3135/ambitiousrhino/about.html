About

Rhino Charge started with a dead phone. Our founder, Aaron Reyes, was a computer science student who kept losing battery halfway through the day, and every power bank he bought either cracked in his backpack or quietly held half the charge the box promised. So he set out to build the one he actually wanted: tough enough to throw in a bag, honest about its capacity, and fast enough to matter between classes.

The rhino fit right away. Rhinos have famously thick skin, they keep moving forward once they start, and a rhino at full speed is called a charge, which is exactly what we sell. Our mascot, the Ambitious Rhino, shows up on every product as the lime lightning horn.

We hold every product in the herd to three promises:

  • Armored shells with rubber corners that survive real backpacks.
  • The capacity on the box is the capacity you actually get.
  • USB-C on everything, so one cable charges the whole herd.
