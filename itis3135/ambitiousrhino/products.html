The Herd

The Rhino Charge herd is six products, each named for a member of the rhino family. Smaller rhinos fit in a pocket, and bigger rhinos power a laptop. Every one ships with our braided Crash Cable and a two-year warranty.

Not sure which rhino you need? Most students carry a Calf or a Bull every day and keep a Black Rhino in their bag for long days. If you edit video or game on a laptop, go straight to the White Rhino.
