Home

Welcome to Rhino Charge, the power company for people who live out of a backpack. We build tough, thick-skinned power banks and chargers that keep your phone, tablet, and laptop alive from your first class to your last shift, and they shrug off the drops and crushed bags that kill cheaper batteries.

Meet the Herd

We keep the herd small on purpose. Six products cover almost everything: a pocket battery, a magnetic phone pack, two laptop-ready power banks, one wall charger for every device, and a folding solar panel for when there's no outlet in sight. Every one of them is built around the same promise of armored shells, honest capacity numbers, and USB-C everywhere.

Finding Your Way Around
