Charge Calculator

The number on a power bank's box only tells you half the story. A 10,000 mAh battery sounds huge until you find out it gives a laptop only about half a charge. The Charge Calculator does the math for you: pick the device you carry and a rhino from the herd, and it tells you how many full charges you'll actually get.

If a rhino can't push enough power for your device, the calculator says so instead of giving you a number that won't hold up. It also points you to the smallest rhino that gives that device a full charge, so you don't carry more weight than you need.

Pick a device and a rhino to see how many charges you get.

How We Do the Math

We turn each rhino's capacity into watt-hours (mAh × 3.7 volts), then count only 70% of it, because some energy is always lost as heat while charging. That usable energy is divided by the size of your device's battery. The Horn 65W and Savanna Solar aren't batteries, so they aren't in the list.
